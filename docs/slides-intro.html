Scope

What this workshop is

✓

Build a foundation

Build a basic, working understanding of generative AI.

✓

Connect it to research

Understand how generative AI can be used in research.

✓

Practice

Work through real examples together.

Scope

What this workshop is not

✗

Not a writing course

Not a place to learn how to write research papers with generative AI.

✗

Not a sales pitch

Not a pitch for AI or any AI software.

✗

Not an open debate

Not a forum for extended debate on the practical, political, and ethical concerns of generative AI, beyond our dedicated ethics conversation.
